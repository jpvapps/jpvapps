Lingomio - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Lingomio is a language-learning application published by JPV Apps.

1. User Accounts

Lingomio does not require users to create an account or sign in to access its lessons.

2. Learning Progress

Lingomio stores learning progress and app preferences locally on the user's device. This information is used to remember completed lessons and personalize the learning experience.

3. Device Permissions and Audio

Lingomio uses text-to-speech functionality to pronounce learning content. Audio processing may depend on the text-to-speech engine installed on the user's device and that engine's own privacy practices.

4. Third-Party Services

Depending on the installed app version, third-party services may process technical information when their features are used. Their privacy practices are governed by their respective policies.

5. Advertising and Payments

If advertising or payment features are introduced or enabled in a future release, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant data processing and service providers.

6. Children's Privacy

Lingomio is designed to support language learning. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of the app where appropriate.

7. Data Retention

Locally stored learning progress generally remains on the device until the app's data is cleared or the app is uninstalled, subject to the device's backup settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy when Lingomio's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact JPV Apps at email.jpvstudios@gmail.com.
